Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install Kaspersky on multiple devices?
+
Yes! Kaspersky Plus covers 3 devices and Kaspersky Premium covers 5 devices. You can use them across Windows, Mac, Android and iOS platforms.
What is the difference between New Buy and Renew?
+
New Buy is for first-time purchasers. Renew is for existing customers whose license is expiring. Renewal plans typically offer a discount compared to new purchases.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
+
Yes, all Kaspersky plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
How do I activate my Kaspersky license?
+
After purchase, you'll receive an activation code via email. Download Kaspersky from the official website, install it, and enter the activation code during setup.
